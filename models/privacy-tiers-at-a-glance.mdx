Every model carries a privacy tier, and the tier decides how private a call to it is. This is the short version: enough to pick a tier and keep working. The full treatment is in the [Privacy](/privacy/overview) section.

## Privacy Follows the Model, Per Call

In the plugin you do not set privacy once for your account. You choose it per prompt by choosing a model, because [the tier follows the model](/privacy/tiers). The same setup can send a throwaway question on a light model and a sensitive one on a Confidential model, with no account-wide switch to remember.

The reason to climb only as high as you need: stronger tiers narrow the set of models available and add overhead. Match the tier to the sensitivity of the call rather than maxing it out.

## The Four Tiers

From lightest to strongest:

| Tier | How you reach it | What it gives you |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Anonymous** | Choose an Anonymous model | Metadata-only retention by default. Nito keeps operational metadata, not your prompt or the reply. |
| **Private** | Choose a Private model | Everything Anonymous gives you, plus the upstream provider held to zero data retention. |
| **Confidential** | Choose a `:confidential` model | Runs inside a sealed environment (a TEE) that produces a signed attestation you can verify. |
| **Encrypted** | Not yet available in the plugin | Adds client-side encryption to the sealed environment. Documented for completeness. |

Two terms worth defining:

- **Zero data retention (ZDR)** means the upstream provider is told, contractually and technically, not to keep your request after serving it.
- **Trusted Execution Environment (TEE)** is a sealed environment the surrounding system cannot read into, which can produce an **attestation**: a signed statement you can check. Think of it as a tamper-evident safe that also hands you a receipt proving it did the work.

## It Never Silently Downgrades

If a model cannot serve a call at its tier, the call **fails with a clear message** rather than dropping to a weaker tier and serving you anyway. A guarantee that quietly weakens is worse than no guarantee, so Nito refuses instead. When a call at a stronger tier succeeds, you know it was served at that tier.

## Anonymous vs Private vs Confidential

- **Anonymous and Private** look the same to you (you just pick the model); the difference is that Private additionally holds the provider to zero data retention.
- **Confidential** is the first tier that gives you a **proof**: Private holds the provider to a promise, while Confidential gives you an attestation you can verify yourself. See [Verify It Yourself](/privacy/tee-attestation).
