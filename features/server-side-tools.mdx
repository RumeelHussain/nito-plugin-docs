Some capabilities are not something the model does on its own; they are work Nito performs during your call and folds into the answer. You turn them on with a flag, and Nito handles the rest.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Web Search" icon="magnifying-glass" href="/features/server-side-tools/web-search">
    Pull fresh information from the web into an answer. Nito decides whether a search is warranted and reports what it did. Flag: `--web-search`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Web Fetch" icon="link" href="/features/server-side-tools/web-fetch">
    Read a specific URL you name in your prompt into the model's context. Flag: `--web-fetch`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Document Parsing" icon="file-lines" href="/features/server-side-tools/file-parser">
    Attach a document with `--file` and Nito extracts its text so the model can read it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How They Fit Together

- **Web search** starts from a question and goes looking for sources.
- **Web fetch** starts from a URL already in your prompt and reads that one page.
- **Document parsing** starts from a file you attach and reads its text.

All three attach to your [`ask`](/commands/ask-and-continue) command (and work inside a [`session`](/commands/session)), and all three work from the [terminal](/cli/ask-and-fusion-from-terminal) too. For attaching images rather than documents, see [Image Input](/features/multimodal/image-input).
