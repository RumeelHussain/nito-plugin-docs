Nito has four plans: **Free**, **Plus**, **Advanced**, and **Max**. Your plan is account-wide, so it covers both the plugin and Nito in your browser. This page is about what each one means for the plugin.

<Note>
Prices are set live and can vary by region and billing period. The snapshot below is a reference. Open **Billing** at [trynito.ai](https://trynito.ai) for the current pricing on your account.
</Note>

## What Matters for the Plugin

Two things need a paid plan:

- **[Fusion](/commands/fusion)** runs one question across two or three models and gives you a single answer. Available from **Plus**.
- **[Full Nito-routed sessions](/commands/session)** power an entire harness session through Nito.

Everything else works on the free allowance until you run out: one-off [`ask`](/commands/ask-and-continue) and [`private`](/commands/private) questions, model selection, and status. See [Free Usage](/plans/free-usage).
