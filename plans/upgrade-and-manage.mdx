title: "Upgrade and Manage"
description: "Where to change plans, how billing is managed, and how a plan change reaches the plugin."
---

Plans are chosen and managed in the **Nito app**, not from inside a chat. The plugin reads your plan; it does not sell or change it. This keeps payment out of your host session and in the billing surface where it belongs.

## Upgrading

Open **Billing** in the Nito app and pick a plan. You can move up the ladder at any time: Free to Plus, Plus to Advanced, or Advanced to Max. Each tier includes everything below it, so upgrading only adds capabilities. For what each tier unlocks, see [What Paid Unlocks](/plans/what-paid-unlocks).

<Note>
Prices are set live and can vary by region and billing period (monthly or annual). Confirm the current price for your account in the app before subscribing.
</Note>

## Payment Options

Billing supports standard card checkout, and **crypto-wallet payment** is available on eligible plans. The exact options shown depend on your account and region; the app presents whatever applies to you at checkout.

## Managing an Existing Subscription

From **Billing** in the app you can:

- Change or cancel your plan.
- Switch between monthly and annual billing.
- View invoices and billing history.
- See your prepaid credit balance, if you use credits.

## How a Change Reaches the Plugin

Your plan and entitlements are read live, so a plan change takes effect for the plugin without reinstalling anything. After upgrading, confirm the new plan on your [status card](/plans/checking-usage) with `status` in a chat, or `nito agent status` from the terminal.

<Note>
If a newly unlocked feature (like [Fusion](/commands/fusion) or a [full session](/commands/session)) still reports as unavailable right after upgrading, re-check `status` to confirm the new plan is reflected, then run the command again. Full sessions additionally require full-session access to be enabled for your organization.
</Note>
