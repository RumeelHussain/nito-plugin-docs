title: "Anonymous"
description: "The lightest privacy level and the default. Standard routing with metadata-only retention, on the broadest set of models."
---

Anonymous is the first rung of the [privacy ladder](/privacy/tiers) and the one most calls use. It is the default level for every provider. It is the right choice for low-sensitivity work where you want the widest model selection and the least overhead, and where you do not need a proof that a specific sealed environment ran your call.

## What Anonymous Is

Anonymous is the baseline posture of the plugin. When you pick a model whose level is Anonymous, your call runs in the Anonymous level. The routing is standard: Nito selects a healthy, eligible, cost-effective backend and serves your request without tying it to a strong privacy boundary. Anonymous is what "ordinary" routing means in practice for a model whose level is Anonymous.

Because Anonymous is the default level across providers, it is also the broadest. Most of the catalog is reachable at Anonymous. Confidential and Encrypted, by contrast, come from specific providers only, so climbing the ladder always narrows the set of models you can pick from.

## What Anonymous Guarantees

The guarantee at Anonymous is **metadata-only retention by default**. Nito keeps operational metadata about the call, not your prompt or the model's reply. Prompts, completions, reasoning, tool payloads, and attachment contents are never persisted, and responses are not stored. Where a chat history exists, it is stored on your side, not by Nito.

What Anonymous does **not** carry is any attestation. By design, an Anonymous model carries no attestation, no sealed-execution provider, and no encryption. So Anonymous gives you a sound default retention posture, but no hardware-backed proof of where or how your call ran. If you need that proof, you climb to [Confidential](/privacy/tiers/confidential). If you need the upstream provider held to no retention at all, you move to [Private](/privacy/tiers/private).

## How to Select Anonymous

You select Anonymous by picking a model whose level is Anonymous. There is no privacy flag on your command: the level follows the model. Browse the catalog with the `models` command to see each model's privacy reach, then set the one you want with `model` or `--model`.

If a model's level is Private instead, picking it lands you in [Private](/privacy/tiers/private), because the model's own level decides which of the two you get. A model's privacy reach is shown when you browse, so you can confirm the level before you set it.

## Trade-offs

- **Lightest protection.** Anonymous gives you metadata-only retention but no upstream zero-data-retention requirement and no attestation. It is the floor, not a guarantee about a provider's internals.
- **Broadest availability.** Because Anonymous is the default level for all providers, it reaches the most models and tends to route to the most backends, which helps latency and availability.
- **No proof to check.** If the value of a call depends on being able to verify where it ran, Anonymous cannot give you that. Pick a Confidential model for an attestation.

## When to Choose Anonymous

Reach for Anonymous when the request is low-sensitivity and you want speed, choice, and simplicity: public or non-confidential prompts, prototyping, internal tooling, or anything where metadata-only retention is enough and you do not need a verifiable execution boundary. Match the level to the sensitivity of the call, and save the stronger rungs for the calls that actually need them.
