---
title: "How Privacy Works"
description: "Two choices decide how private a call is: what leaves your harness, and the privacy level of the model that answers."
---

Privacy on the plugin is not one setting you turn on for your account. It comes from two choices you make, per prompt.

1. **What leaves your harness.** Claude Code and Codex normally send your prompts to their own model provider. Nito commands go to Nito instead, and [`private`](/commands/private) keeps a turn away from your harness's provider entirely.
2. **The privacy level of the model that answers.** Every model in the catalog carries a privacy level. You get the level of the model you choose.

## Who Can See What

The thing worth understanding is who sees a given question.

- **Your harness**, Claude Code or Codex, is the program on your machine. In normal use it sends your prompts to its own model provider, which is Anthropic or OpenAI.
- **Nito** is the service the plugin calls.

When you run a Nito command, that question goes to Nito. Whether your harness also sees it depends on the command.

- [`ask`](/commands/ask-and-continue) runs inside your harness conversation, so it travels upstream with the rest of it.
- [`private`](/commands/private) does not. The question and the answer stay out of what your harness sends.

[What Each Command Protects](/privacy/what-each-command-protects) lays this out command by command.

## Private and Session Keep Different Things

Both run the whole session through Nito, so neither conversation reaches Anthropic or OpenAI. What separates them is what stays on your machine afterwards.

- [`private`](/commands/private), **with no question after it**, opens a session that keeps nothing. No prompt history, no transcript, nothing to resume. Close it and there is nothing left to find.
- [`session`](/commands/session) keeps prompt history, transcripts, and resume state on your machine, so you can come back to the work. That record is yours, and anyone with access to your machine can read it.

Either way, the work itself lives in your workspace like any other work. Running a task through Nito keeps the conversation away from Anthropic and OpenAI. It does not hide the files that task wrote from the next ordinary session you open in that workspace.

## The Level Follows the Model

There is no privacy toggle in the plugin. Each model in the catalog carries a privacy level, and you get that level by choosing that model. Picking a more private model is how you get a more private call.

Because the level is a property of the model, it cannot drift.

## The Four Levels, Briefly

A ladder. Each rung keeps everything below it and adds one thing.

| Level | Adds |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Anonymous** | Metadata-only retention by default. Broadest choice of models. |
| **Private** | The upstream provider is held to zero data retention. |
| **Confidential** | Your call runs in a sealed environment you can verify with a signed attestation. |
| **Encrypted** | Your data is encrypted to that sealed environment. Not available in the plugin yet. |

Full detail, including what each protects and what it costs you, is in [The Four Levels](/privacy/tiers).

## Attested, Not Asserted

The two strongest levels replace a promise with something you can check. A Confidential call runs inside a sealed environment that produces a **signed attestation**, and you can verify that attestation yourself rather than take the claim on trust.

## Metadata Only, by Default

Even the lightest level is not a free-for-all. Nito keeps operational metadata about a call, not its contents. Your questions, the replies, reasoning, tool payloads, and attachment contents are used to serve the request and are not stored.

[Data Retention and Visibility](/privacy/data-retention-and-visibility) covers exactly what is kept and who can see what.
