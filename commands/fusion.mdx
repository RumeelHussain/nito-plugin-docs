---
title: "Fusion"
description: "Send one prompt to two or three models, then get a synthesized answer that reconciles them and shows where they disagree."
---

`fusion` sends one prompt to **two or three** models at once, then runs a synthesis pass that reconciles their answers into one and shows you where they agreed and disagreed. It is the command to reach for on a contested decision, where a single model's confident answer would hide that the question is genuinely open.

<Note>
Fusion requires a **paid plan**. See [What Paid Unlocks](/plans/what-paid-unlocks).
</Note>

## Syntax

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Claude Code">
    ```text
    /nito:fusion [--file <path>]... [--web-search off|auto|on] [--web-fetch=off|on] [--judge <model-id>] <model-id> <model-id> [model-id] -- <prompt>
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Codex">
    ```text
    $nito:fusion [--file <path>]... [--web-search off|auto|on] [--web-fetch=off|on] [--judge <model-id>] <model-id> <model-id> [model-id] -- <prompt>
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

You name **two or three** participant models as positional arguments. Fewer than two or more than three is rejected with a clear message, and the models must be distinct.

### Options

| Option | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| `<model-id> <model-id> [model-id]` | The two or three participant models. |
| `--judge <model-id>` | The model that runs the synthesis. Defaults to one of the participants. |
| `--file <path>` | Attach a local file to every participant. Repeatable. |
| `--web-search` / `--web-fetch` | Control server tools per participant. See [Web Search](/features/server-side-tools/web-search). |

## How Privacy Tiers Mix

Participants must share a compatible privacy level:

- **Anonymous and Private can mix** in one Fusion.
- **Confidential must stay in a Confidential-only Fusion.** Combining a Confidential model with a lower tier is refused, so a synthesis never quietly downgrades the privacy of a Confidential participant.

## Reading the Results

A Fusion result has two parts:

1. **Each participant, listed separately**, with the model, timing, tokens, and whether web search was used.
2. **A synthesis** that gives a single reconciled answer, and, importantly, a **"where the candidates disagree"** section that names the split and which way it leans.

That disagreement map is the point: you see *that* a decision is contested and exactly where, instead of one model's opinion presented as settled.

## Example

```text
/nito:fusion anthropic/claude-sonnet-5 anthropic/claude-haiku-4.5 google/gemini-3.5-flash -- For a new multi-tenant SaaS API, should auth be stateless JWTs or server-side sessions? Answer in 3 sentences.
```

The result lists each model's answer, then a synthesized recommendation with the tradeoff and a note on where the three models diverged.
