---

`private` is the plugin's flagship. Unlike `ask`, a private prompt and its answer are kept **out of your host model's request**, your host's upstream never sees them. The answer comes from a Nito model and is shown back to you as a blocked turn.

## Run It

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Claude Code">
    ```text
    /nito:private Review this snippet for a security bug: <your code>
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Codex">
    ```text
    $nito:private Review this snippet for a security bug: <your code>
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What Just Happened

The plugin intercepted the command before your host ran its own inference, sent a **prompt-only** request to a Nito model through the local helper, and returned the answer as a blocked result. The private prompt and the Nito answer were **excluded from your host model's request**, so they never became part of the host's context.

The result is labeled `Session mode: Incognito` and shows the model and privacy tier that answered.

<Warning>
The private guarantee depends on your host's hooks being **enabled and trusted**. If hooks are disabled, the command cannot intercept the turn. Do not submit private data with hooks off. On Codex, this is the one-time `/hooks` trust step from install.
</Warning>

## What Stays in the Path

Private keeps your prompt out of the *host model's request*. It does not remove everyone from the path: your local host process, your operating system, the Nito gateway, and the selected model provider are still involved. That boundary is covered in full on [The Trust Boundary](/privacy/trust-boundary).

## Open a Full Incognito Session

Run `private` with **no prompt** to go further than a single turn:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Claude Code">
    ```text
    /nito:private
    ```
    Opens a fresh, fully Nito-routed session that keeps nothing, no prompt history, no transcript, no resume state. Your original session is untouched and receives only a launch result.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Codex">
    ```text
    $nito:private
    ```
    Reports that a no-prompt private **session** is not yet available on Codex (coming soon), without calling any model. For now, use `$nito:private <prompt>` for one private turn, or `$nito:session` for a persistent Nito-routed session.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
