---
title: "What the Nito Plugin Is"
description: "Call open-weight and frontier models from inside Claude Code and Codex, at a privacy level you choose, without changing your harness."
---

The Nito plugin lets you call open-weight models like Kimi K3, GLM 5.2, DeepSeek, and Qwen, plus frontier models like GPT, Claude, and Gemini, from inside **Claude Code** and **Codex**. No second tool to learn, and no provider keys to collect.

Your harness keeps running its own model. Ordinary messages never touch Nito, and a call goes through Nito only on the commands you type. Nothing in your existing setup changes.

Every Nito call runs at a named privacy level, and the level comes with the model you pick. Frontier models run anonymized, so the provider gets your question without learning who is asking. Open-weight models go further: retention switched off upstream, and stronger levels running inside attested hardware that hands back proof of what ran. Nito keeps neither the question nor the answer.

The commands are the whole surface. In Claude Code they are `/nito:…`; in Codex they are `$nito:…`. Everything else in your session works exactly as before.

## What You Can Do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Use any model" icon="shuffle">
    `/nito:ask` sends your question to whichever model you pick from the Nito catalog and puts the answer in your session. Name a model per call with `--model`, or set a default once and leave it alone.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ask without it going upstream" icon="user-secret">
    Put a question after `/nito:private` and it goes through Nito instead. The answer lands inline, in the session you are already in. Your harness never sends it upstream, and Nito keeps no copy.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Work in a separate session" icon="window">
    `/nito:session` opens a second session in your workspace where a model from the Nito catalog does all the work, and keeps your history so you can come back to it. Run `/nito:private` with no question and you get the same thing, disposable: nothing is saved and closing it leaves nothing behind.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ask several models at once" icon="layer-group">
    `/nito:fusion` sends one question to two or three models, then gives you a single answer and shows you where they agreed and disagreed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## It Is the Same Harness

The plugin sits *alongside* Claude Code or Codex rather than replacing either one. Same harness, same configuration, same tools. It does not swap out the model running your session, rewrite your settings, or send your ordinary prompts anywhere. When you run a Nito command, that one call goes to Nito. When you don't, nothing does.

## A Privacy Level on Every Call

Pick a model and you have picked its privacy level. The plugin offers three today, up to Confidential.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Anonymous">
    Frontier models, routed so the provider gets your question without learning who is asking.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Private">
    Open-weight models, with retention switched off upstream, so the provider does not store your question or the answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Confidential">
    The model runs inside attested hardware. Nito checks the attestation before you see the answer, and blocks the response if the check fails.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

A privacy level never silently weakens. If you ask for a stronger level than a model supports, the call is refused with a clear message rather than served at a weaker one. The full ladder is covered in [Privacy](/privacy/levels).

## When You Don't Want It Going Upstream

`/nito:ask` is an ordinary turn in your session, so it travels upstream with the rest of your conversation like everything else you type.

`/nito:private` doesn't. Your question goes through Nito instead, the answer comes back into your session, and your harness never sends either one upstream. Nito keeps no copy.

It does two things depending on what follows it. A question after `/nito:private` gets answered inline. Nothing after it opens a private session that saves nothing.
