The Nito plugin adds a second, private AI layer inside **Claude Code** and **Codex**. Without changing the model that runs your session, it lets you call other models through Nito (open-source and frontier) and attach a privacy level to every one of those calls. Your host stays primary: ordinary messages never touch Nito, and Nito runs only when you explicitly ask it to.

Think of it as a set of focused commands you invoke on purpose. In Claude Code they are `/nito:…`; in Codex they are `$nito:…`. Everything else in your session works exactly as before.

## What You Can Do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Ask a second model" icon="comment-question">
    `/nito:ask` gets a one-shot second opinion from a Nito model inside your current chat, then labels which model and privacy level answered.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fuse several models" icon="layer-group">
    `/nito:fusion` sends one prompt to two or three models, then synthesizes a single answer and shows you where they agreed and disagreed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Run a private prompt" icon="user-secret">
    `/nito:private` answers from a Nito model in an incognito turn your host's upstream never sees, the prompt and answer stay out of the host model's request.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open a Nito-routed session" icon="window">
    `/nito:session` opens a separate, fully Nito-routed session in your workspace, powered by a model you choose from the Nito catalog.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## It Never Changes Your Host's Own Model

The plugin sits *alongside* Claude Code or Codex. It does not swap out the model running your session, rewrite your configuration, or route your ordinary prompts anywhere. When you run a Nito command, that one call goes to Nito; when you don't, nothing does. Your host model, its tools, and its settings are untouched.

## A Privacy Level on Every Call

Every Nito call carries a privacy tier, and you choose it by the model you pick. The tier follows the model.

- **Frontier models** run in an **anonymized** tier: broad model access with a metadata-only retention posture.
- **Open-source models** can reach deeper tiers, including **Confidential**, where your call runs inside attested hardware and you can verify it ran there.

A privacy level never silently weakens. If you request a stronger tier than a model supports, the call is refused with a clear message rather than served at a weaker level. The full ladder (Anonymous, Private, Confidential, and Encrypted) is covered in [Privacy](/privacy/tiers).

<Note>
The **no-trace** guarantee belongs to `/nito:private` specifically. A regular `/nito:ask` is a normal turn in your host chat, so the host model can see that exchange. Use Private when the prompt must stay out of the host's request.
</Note>
