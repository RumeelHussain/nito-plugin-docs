Questions about cost and limits. See [Plans and Billing](/plans/what-paid-unlocks) for the full picture.

#### Is the plugin free to use?

Eligible free accounts get a monthly allowance of plugin requests. When it runs out, upgrade to keep going. See [Free Usage](/plans/free-usage).

#### What does a paid plan unlock?

Higher limits plus the paid-only capabilities like [Fusion](/commands/fusion) and full Nito-routed [sessions](/commands/session). See [What Paid Unlocks](/plans/what-paid-unlocks).

#### How do I check my usage?

Run `status` inside a chat for your plan and usage card, including any free-allowance notice. See [Checking Usage](/plans/checking-usage).

#### What counts against my free allowance?

Model-backed commands like `ask` and `private` each count once. Browsing with `models`, checking `status`, or opening the menu do not count. See [Free Usage](/plans/free-usage).
